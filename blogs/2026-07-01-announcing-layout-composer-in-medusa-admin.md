Medusa Admin introduces a Layout Composer feature that lets users reorder, hide, and save the arrangement of nearly every part of the dashboard. The tool, originating from a community contribution, allows customization of pages, sidebars, and top bar elements, with options to set personal preferences or establish global defaults for team consistency.
